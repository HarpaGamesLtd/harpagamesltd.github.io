Italek Ltd is responsible for the data described here (the data controller). You can reach us at harpagamesltd@gmail.com.

1. Data stored only on your device

Your progress (coins, gems, upgrades, levels, purchases you made, sound and vibration settings) is saved in a file on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases (for example removing ads or buying gems) are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. We only receive confirmation of what was bought, so we can deliver it.

4. Ads

The game is free and shows ads: short ads between levels, and optional videos you can choose to watch for a reward. Ads are delivered through AppLovin MAX, which works with other ad networks, including Google AdMob. These companies may collect the data described above under their own privacy policies:

  • AppLovin privacy policy and its list of partners
  • How Google uses data in ads
  • Google privacy policy (also covers Firebase Analytics)

Buying "No Ads" turns off the ads between levels. Optional reward videos stay available.

5. Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: when you first open the game we ask for your consent before analytics and personalised ads. If you decline, analytics stays off and any ads shown are not personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings, then "Privacy choices".
  • iOS: the game asks for permission before tracking you across other companies' apps and websites. You can change this later in your iPhone's Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.
  • United States: you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising by contacting us at the address below. Where the game offers an opt-out setting, you can also use that.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google, then Ads).

6. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

We rely on your consent for analytics and personalised advertising, and on our legitimate interest in running and protecting the game for non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and fixing problems.

7. How long we keep data

Analytics data linked to an app instance ID is deleted automatically after the retention period set in Google Analytics, which is at most 14 months. Ad partners keep data according to their own policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we don't know who you are, please send your app instance ID or advertising ID with your request so we can find the data. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. International transfers

Our service providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. They use legal safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Children

Black Hole Evolution is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has used the game and data was collected, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Important changes will also be shown in the game.
